Pulling satellite imagery
How this works: Images come directly from NASA's GIBS (Global Imagery Browse Services) — a free, public satellite imagery service, no API key required. The True Color view is a real photo-like MODIS/Terra image; the NDVI view colorizes vegetation density (green = healthy, tan = sparse/bare soil) computed from near-infrared reflectance. "best" auto-selects the most recent cloud-free pass available for your coordinates.
