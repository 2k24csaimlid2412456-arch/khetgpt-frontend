Weather Risk

5-day forecast-based disease risk for your field

Reading the sky
How this works: KhetGPT pulls a live 5-day forecast (temperature + humidity, every 3 hours) for your coordinates from OpenWeatherMap. It flags high risk when extended periods of high humidity (>75%) combine with warm temperatures (20–30°C) — the exact conditions fungal diseases like blight and rust thrive in. Default coordinates above are set to Lucknow, UP — change them to your own field's location for an accurate reading.
